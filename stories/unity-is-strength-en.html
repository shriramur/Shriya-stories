Unity is Strength

Ramu and the Mischievous Mice 🐭🐜

Story imagined by: Shriya ✨

Scroll down ⬇️

1
At Ramu's house, Amma made delicious laddus. "Eat them when you're back from school, Ramu!" she said, tucking them safely into a box. Ramu happily went off to school. 🍬🏫
2
While Ramu was at school... some mischievous mice snuck into the house! They opened the box and gobbled up every single laddu! 🐭🍬
3
The mice caused all kinds of mischief — they climbed onto Ramu's school bag, and even jumped right over Amma's head! "Aiyo!" It was trouble for everyone! 😱
4
Appa angrily ran to chase the mice away... but oh no! He tripped and fell, hurting his hand! And the mouse got away anyway! 🤕
5
Amma, upset, scolded everyone. Appa's hand still hurt. Ramu burst into tears. The whole house was sunk in sadness. 😢
6
Just then, Ramu had an idea! "We shouldn't run away scared! If we all work together, we can chase the mice out!" The household ants marched up in a line: "We'll help too!" they said. 💡🐜
7
Everyone became one team! Appa grabbed a big broom, Amma banged on a pot, and Ramu beat a drum — Bang! Bang! 🧹🥁
8
Hearing all that noise, the mice trembled with fright! "Run! Run!" they squeaked, and dashed clean out of the house through the door! 🐭💨
9
Afterwards, everyone joined together to clean the house. The ants carried away every last crumb of scattered laddu. The house sparkled like new! 🧽🐜✨
10
Amma made a fresh batch of laddus! Ramu, Amma, Appa, and their ant friends — everyone celebrated together with a feast! 🎉🍬

🌟 The Moral of the Story 🌟

No matter how big the problem,
anything is easy when everyone stands together!

"Unity is strength!" 🐜🤝
