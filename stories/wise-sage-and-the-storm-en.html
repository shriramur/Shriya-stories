The Wise Sage and the Storm

A Story of Calm and Courage 🌙

For Shriya ❤️

Scroll down ⬇️

1
Once upon a time, long, long ago, there was a kind old sage. He lived in a little hut in the forest, with a big wooden door. He wore a special watch on his wrist. Tick tock, tick tock! Birds sang outside his window every morning. Chirp chirp!
2
Later that day, the sky began to change. Gray clouds rolled in, one by one. The wind started to whisper, and the birds flew home early. "Something is coming," thought the sage, watching the sky.
3
Then the wind went WHOOSH! The trees swayed side to side. The leaves went rustle rustle! The big door went creak creak! "Oh my!" said the sage. But he did not cry. He took a deep breath — in and out — and stayed calm.
4
Creak! The door shook harder and harder. A little rain crept in under it. "My hut needs a rest from this wind," said the sage gently. "I know just the place." He wrapped his shawl tight and stepped out into the storm.
5
So the sage walked, and walked, and walked, all the way up a big, tall mountain. Step, step, step! At last, he found a cozy little cave. "Just right for me!" he smiled. He curled up safe and warm inside, and the rain went pitter patter outside.
6
Outside, the storm rumbled on through the night. But inside the little cave, it was warm and snug. The sage closed his eyes and slept soundly. By morning, the wind had gone quiet, and only soft sunlight peeked into the cave.
7
As he set off for home, a big tiger walked by. Soft paws, swishy tail. The sage stood very, very still — like a statue! Not a wiggle, not a giggle. Shhh... The tiger sniffed the air, then walked away. Phew!
8
With the tiger gone, the sage walked on. The sun peeked out and said, "Good morning!" The birds sang again. Chirp chirp! The sage walked home, step by step, with a big happy smile. He opened his wooden door — creak — and it was safe and cozy inside.

🌟 The Moral of the Story 🌟

Stay calm, be brave,
and everything will be okay.
