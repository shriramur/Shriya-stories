Pink Pink and Shanmukhi

A Story of Fire, Friends, and a New Home 🐰🐑

For Shriya ❤️

Scroll down ⬇️

1
Pink Pink the rabbit lived happily in the Whispering Woods. Every day, she hopped through the green meadow, sniffing out juicy orange carrots. Hop, hop, hop! Life was sweet and simple.
2
One summer, the heat turned the forest dry and crackly. Then, one day, thick gray smoke rose over the tall trees. "Oh no!" The forest animals looked back, their hearts pounding with fright.
3
The wildfire spread fast across the dry woods. Crackle! Crackle! Flames burned through the bushes and swallowed Pink Pink's cozy underground burrow. Everyone had to run to safety.
4
When the fire finally died down, the forest was scorched, dark, and empty. Pink Pink sat alone on a rock, surrounded by ash. Sniff... sniff... She cried softly for her lost home and all her carrots.
5
From across the ridge, Shanmukhi the lamb spotted the smoke and came rushing over. She wrapped Pink Pink in a warm, woolly hug. "Don't worry, I'm here," she said softly. Ding-a-ling went her little golden bell.
6
Shanmukhi knew Pink Pink needed more than a hug. She called out to her friends — Chippy the crow, Simba the lion cub, Kavin the fawn, and Foxy the fox. "Let's help Pink Pink together!" Everyone gathered on the hill, ready to help.
7
The six friends set off, leaving the burnt woods behind. They walked and walked toward the misty green hills far away, searching for a brand-new, safe home for Pink Pink.
8
Soon a deep, dry ravine blocked their path. Uh-oh! But Kavin and Simba worked together and pushed a big fallen log across the gap, making a bridge for everyone to cross.
9
Thick smoke still hung near the mountain pass, and no one could see the way. But Foxy the fox sniffed the air with his clever nose. Sniff, sniff! "This way, friends — follow me!"
10
At last, the friends reached the top of the mountain. Below them lay a vibrant, green valley, glowing in the sunshine — untouched by fire or smoke. "We found it!" they all cheered.
11
Chippy the crow flew ahead to look around. Suddenly he spotted something amazing — a huge field bursting with big, sweet, freshly grown carrots! "Down here, everyone!" he cawed.
12
Pink Pink's eyes lit up with joy! She dug up armfuls of carrots and shared them with every single friend. They sat together in a circle, munching and laughing. Crunch, crunch!
13
Together, the friends got to work. Shanmukhi carried soft moss, Foxy placed sturdy twigs, and everyone pitched in. Soon, Pink Pink had a brand-new, cozy burrow, right in the heart of the valley.
14
That night, under a sky full of stars, the friends caught twinkling fireflies in little glass jars. They sang songs together, snuggled up close, and celebrated their brave journey. Twinkle, twinkle!

🌟 The Moral of the Story 🌟

Even when fire takes away a home, true friendship helps you build a better one.
Sharing and caring make every journey worth it! 🌈💛
