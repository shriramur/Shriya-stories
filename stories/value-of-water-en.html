The Value of Water

A Farmer's Story 🌾

For Shriya ❤️

Scroll down ⬇️

1
In a beautiful village lived a farmer. He had lots of pets — a cow, a goat, and hens. Everyone lived happily together. 🐄🐐🐔
2
Back then, water was plentiful. Not knowing its true worth, the farmer wasted it — he left the tap running and poured far more water than he needed! 🚰💦
3
Days passed... there was not a single cloud in the sky! "When will the rain come?" the farmer and his animals wondered, staring up at the sky. Even the empty pots waited. 🌤️👀
4
One year, the rains failed and a drought struck the village. The ponds, puddles, and lakes all dried up. The farmer and his animals struggled badly without water. 😢☀️
5
There wasn't even water to drink! The farmer walked all the way to a faraway village, carrying pots balanced on a pole across his shoulders, to bring back a little water. It was so hard! 🚶🏺
6
The farmer dug into the earth and drilled a borewell. Water gushed up from deep below! "The water is here!" everyone cheered with joy. ⛏️💧
7
That night, the farmer remembered the old days. "Oh no! How much water I wasted! I left the tap running all those times!" he thought, filled with regret. 😔💭
8
The next morning, the farmer prayed to Lord Varuna, the god of rain. "From now on, I will use water carefully," he promised. 🙏💧
9
Good rain fell, day after day! All the ponds filled up. A rainbow appeared! The frogs sang, the fish leapt about. The farmer and his pets danced with joy. 🌧️🌈😄
10
From that day on, the farmer used water carefully. He saved rainwater, watered his plants drop by drop, and gave his animals only what they needed. Not a single drop was wasted! He even taught the whole village to save water too. 💧🌱✅

🌟 The Moral of the Story 🌟

Water is very precious to us!
It is best used carefully, and never wasted.

Every single drop is a treasure! 💧✨
